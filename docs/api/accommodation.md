# API Specification: `accommodation`

> Owner: `@P2` (shared with `@P3` for UI)  
> Base Path: `/api/v1/accommodation`  
> Status: `APPROVED`

---

## 1. Overview
The Accommodation module provides an explainable, deterministic planner for the Aarohan festival (EPINOIA 2026, IIM Indore). It manages on-campus hostels (710 beds across 200 rooms in Boys and Girls blocks) and 6 external properties (740 beds), evaluating peak simultaneous demand (~1,601 beds) against hard safety, accessibility, and privacy rules.

The engine operates with a hard-filter-first strategy, pure functional core (`engines/accommodation`), and database integration wrapper (`services/accommodation`). Candidate plans generated by the engine (`A`, `B`, `C`) are candidate drafts and NOT live until explicitly approved by the Festival Director.

### 2.5 `GET /api/v1/accommodation/plans/{id}/shortages` (and alias `/shortages`)
- **Description**: Breakdown of unplaced participants classified by the fixed blocking reason taxonomy with counterfactuals.
- **Taxonomy Codes**:
  - `HARD_ACCESSIBILITY`: Wheelchair or ground-floor requirement without eligible accessible room.
  - `GENDER_OR_HOUSING_UNKNOWN`: Non-binary, prefer-not-to-say, or UNKNOWN gender ineligible for gendered blocks without private/neutral option.
  - `TRANSPORT_RESTRICTED`: Late arrival or early event prevents placement at distant or limited-transport property.
  - `NO_ELIGIBLE_PROPERTY`: Property gender suitability or accessibility is unverified, or property full.
  - `BUDGET`: External cost exceeds budget ceiling (suppressed in budget-unset mode).
  - `MISSING_INFO`: Key stay interval, guardian approval, or escort status unconfirmed.
  - `CAPACITY`: All compliant beds occupied during the stay window.
- **Response `200 OK`**:
```json
{
```

### 2.10 `GET /api/v1/accommodation/reports/{report_name}`
- **Description**: Download CSV reports generated by the accommodation engine.
- **Supported Reports**:
  - `accommodation_plan_A.csv`: Cohesion-first plan stay assignments.
  - `accommodation_plan_B.csv`: Cost-first plan stay assignments.
  - `accommodation_plan_C.csv`: Balanced plan stay assignments.
  - `shortage_report.csv`: Unplaced participants with taxonomy reason codes and missing attributes.
  - `accommodation_summary.csv`: Plan comparison metrics table.
  - `bed_demand_timeline.csv`: Hourly confirmed demand, possible demand, and supply.
- **Permissions**: `accommodation:export`
