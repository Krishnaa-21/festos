# FestOS: findings from the actual data pack

Generated by `festos_engine.py` (run: `python festos_engine.py <pack_dir> <out_dir>`). Every number below is reproducible from the CSVs in this folder.
Assumptions are listed in `assumptions.csv`; none are hidden.

## 1. Reconciliation: 2,836 vs "about 2,400"
- `contingents.csv` declared strengths sum to **2,842**, and the file has **2,836** records. The pack agrees with itself; the "2,400" in the narrative looks stale. Plan on 2,836 and keep 2,400 as a flagged mismatch.
- 7 contingents disagree with their declared size (C05 -8, C18 -5, C32 -7, C27 +7, C09 +5, C02 +1, C03 +1): `contingent_reconciliation.csv`.
- Duplicates are ambiguous: every contact number is unique, and names repeat because the name pool is small. Only **7 likely** and **305 possible** duplicate pairs (name+college+age+gender+size+diet+times scoring): `duplicate_candidates.csv`. Nothing is auto-merged.
- 3 records depart before they arrive (held out of allocation). Gender is blank for 417, "Prefer not to say" for 430, non-binary for 415. Diet is blank for 394 (treated as UNKNOWN, never "no restriction").
- Leaders and escorts in `contingents.csv` are almost never in the participant file (3 of 38 each): ~70 people who need rooms are not in demand figures.
- 279 participants are aged 17 (220 request accommodation).

## 2. Accommodation: the hard part is structural
- 2,144 request a bed. Supply is 710 on-campus + 740 external = 1,450.
- **Peak simultaneous demand is 1,601 (30 Oct 18:00)**, so even perfect packing is ~150 beds short.
- On-campus has 474 boys' beds and 236 girls' beds and **no gender-neutral rooms**. At peak: 474 female, 417 male, **710 non-binary / not stated / blank**. Rule 8 forbids guessing, so those 710 are never auto-placed in a gendered block.
- **Only 10 rooms (26 beds) are accessible**, against 72 wheelchair requests at peak (plus 80 ground-floor). External properties carry no accessibility data, so hard-need people are NOT placed there.
- Result (both plans): **861 on campus, 920 external, 360 unplaced** (109 are hard-need; 241 have unconfirmed gender), 71 minors in external stays flagged for guardian approval, total external cost about Rs 33.2 lakh, 36 of 38 contingents split.
- Cohesion-first vs cost-first differ by only about Rs 3,550 because availability, not preference, forces nearly every placement. The honest trade-off is shown in `accommodation_summary_by_contingent_*.csv`.
- Verified on the output: 0 gender-block violations, 0 wheelchair/ground-floor violations, 0 room or property over-capacity at any arrival instant, 0 minors or late-night arrivals in the limited-transport property.
- What would close the gap: confirm housing preferences of the unconfirmed group (spare boys' beds exist off-peak), verify accessibility of external properties, and add a gender-neutral block.

## 3. Schedule: `events.csv` has no times or venues, so one is generated
- `venues.csv` has no feature tags; the venue-to-requirement map is an assumption (editable in `VENUE_OK`).
- Minimum need with perfect packing is about 218 venue-hours of 360 available (windows are 14h/14h/12h per venue). Participants are only partly on site at any time (13% arrive 31 Oct 22:00, ~12% leave before 31 Oct 09:00).
- Prototype result: 172 sessions, 0 venue overlaps, but **987 of 5,580 registrations (about 18%) have no prelim slot and 28 semi/final sessions are unplaced**. Much of this is my greedy heuristic fragmenting heats into tiny ones; the next step is a proper clustering or CP-SAT pass. Treat the schedule as a conflict-detecting draft, not a final plan. Unscheduled entrants are listed with reasons, never dropped.
- Wave L (1 Nov morning) lets late arrivals compete; assumed they join finals as wildcards. That is a fairness decision for the director.
- No judge data exists anywhere in the pack, so judge conflicts cannot be detected yet. 1,810 team-event registrations have no team_id.
- Volunteers: concurrent sessions need up to ~56 more people than the core team has on shift (`resource_load_timeline.csv`).
- Equipment: **cameras are short** (0 good of 31; 3 needed at once). Tables rely on "needs check" units.
